NTP ( Network Time Protocol) is used to ensure time on all your devices are time synchronized. 

Make sure package ntp is installed. click on System, Package. 
pool.ntp.org. click System, NTP Server, enable Broadcast, this will send out a packet onto the network to say 'I'm a time server'. Next configure NTP Client, you need a client that client is connected and synchronized with the router and will redistribute the time to other devices. Click NTP Client, click Enabled and enter domain name in Primary NTP Server: 0.pool.ntp.org. Enter Secondary NTP Server: 1.pool.ntp.org. It will automatically resolve the IP address. Enter the IP address of this router on the other devices and it will get the time of this router.
