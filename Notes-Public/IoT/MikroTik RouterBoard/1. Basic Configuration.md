Connecting your ethernet cable into port 2 to 5. The reason we don't use 1 is because by default is not allowed to configure the router, because of firewall rules. Plug the ethernet cable on port 2-5 on one end and on the other end your computer. 

Connect power cable into the power jack 

Download WinBox, it's going to configure the router. 

## Note
I had a problem connecting. I resolved it by going to the control panel, change adapter settings and looked at realtek adapter if it was connected. Turns out the cable didn't connect with the computer properly.

Quick Set config. Address Acuqisition: choose Automatic. Set IP Address: 192.168.5.1. Usually they use 192.168.0.1, we just don't want a conflict with IPs just to be safe. DHCP range 192.168.5.10-192.168.5.254. Choose NAT. Click apply.

Wireless. You'll see wlan1, click on it. Mode: ap bridge. Frequency: auto. SSID, name of your access point, leave it as it is. Click ok. Click on Security profile. Change the mode to dynamic keys. WPA2 PSK, it's strong, more secure. Key, remember to remember it.

System - Reboot, click yes. The router will reboot, you'll see wifi light flashing. Plug your cable from your modem into port 1 and now you'll be able to browse the internet through your mikrotik router.
